VIMMIK BRAND KIT
================

logo/svg      Vector logos. Use these wherever possible (web, print, design tools).
logo/png      Transparent PNGs. Marks at 2048 px, lockups at 3000 px wide.
                *-light-bg  : for white or light backgrounds (navy V)
                *-dark-bg   : for dark backgrounds (ice V)
                *-mono-*    : one-colour versions
colors        brand-tokens.css and colors.json
fonts         Orbitron, DM Sans, IBM Plex Mono, Montserrat (SIL Open Font License, see each OFL.txt)
social        Profile picture, LinkedIn banners, post / square / story templates
guidelines    The visual pages of the brand guidelines

The full rules live in the VIMMIK Brand Guidelines document.
Never redraw, retype, stretch or recolour the logo.
